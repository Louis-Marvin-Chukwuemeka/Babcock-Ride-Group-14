Project Title: Babcock Ride

1. Product Overview

Question: What is the product about

Babcock ride is a mobile application that helps Babcock student find and book nearby rides. They enter their destination, see available riders and order rides using their mobile devices

2. Problem statement

Question: what problem we are solving?

Students sometimes find it difficult to get transportation around Babcock University. They spend time looking for available drivers and negotiating prices.

3. Bussiness objective

Question:What does the bussiness want?

- We want  to ba able to make trnsportation on babcock easy for student
-  To reduce wasting time 
- To provide affordable transportation.
- To make booking rides faster.

4. Target Users

Questions:Who will use the product

 Students: Those who need transportation.
 Drivers: Those who transport students.
 Administrators: Those who manage the application.

5. Stakeholders

Question:Who is involved and affected
 The stakeholders are 
- Babcock University students.
- Transport drivers.
- University management.
- Our application developers.
- System administrators.

 6. User stories

Question:What do the users want to accomplish?
- As a student, I want to see nearby drivers so I can get a ride.
- As a student, I want to see the price before booking.
- As a driver, I want to receive ride requests from students.
- As a driver, I want to accept or reject ride requests.
